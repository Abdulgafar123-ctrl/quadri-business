Our journey

A small idea became a brand built around meaningful gifting.

Glimpaxe Momento Souvenirs started in 2021 with a simple belief: the best souvenirs do more than mark an event—they help people remember how the moment felt.

What began with a small working team has grown into a creative gifting business focused on customized souvenirs, personalized gifts and corporate packages. Our approach combines design thinking, practical planning and careful presentation.
