Before you enquire

Questions people often ask about our services.

Can I request a combination of different products?

Yes. We can help you combine suitable products into a coordinated souvenir or gift package based on your event and budget.

Can you customize products with names or logos?

Yes. Personalization can include names, messages, logos, colours, themes and other relevant event or brand details, depending on the product.

Do you handle large quantities?

Yes. Bulk and event orders are part of our service. Tell us your approximate quantity early so we can plan the recommendation appropriately.

What if I don't know what product to choose?

That is completely fine. Tell us the event, audience, quantity and budget and we can recommend suitable directions for you to consider.

Can businesses order branded gifts?

Yes. We support corporate gifting for clients, employees, partners, conferences, appreciation campaigns and other business occasions.

How do I get a quotation?

Use the Get a Quote page or message us directly on WhatsApp with your event details. We can then discuss the right options and requirements.
